Let's QuizQuiz night on video

Get ready for quiz night

Join from a computer, a phone or the TV. Everyone ends up in the same quiz room. The quizmaster will give you the password; you only type it once.

All the ways to join

Windows computer

This device
  1. Download and open Lets-Quiz-Setup.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. (The app is fine; it just isn't registered with Microsoft.)
  3. Let's Quiz opens by itself and puts a shortcut on your desktop.
Download for Windows

Mac

This device
  1. Download Lets-Quiz-Mac, open it and drag Let's Quiz into Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. The first time, the Mac says it can't check the app: click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Say OK when it asks for the camera and microphone.
Download for Mac

Amazon Fire TV Stick

  1. On the Fire Stick, install Downloader from the Appstore.
  2. In Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options, allow Downloader to install apps. (No Developer options? Go to About and click the device name seven times.)
  3. In Downloader, type tinyurl.com/letsquiztv and install. Let's Quiz TV shows the quiz full screen.

Phone, tablet or any browser

This device
  1. Nothing to install: just open gathercall.uk/quiz.
  2. To keep it handy, use Add to Home Screen (Share menu on iPhone, ⋮ menu on Android).
Open the quiz

On a computer the app and the browser work the same way; the app just keeps quiz night one click away. Allow the camera and microphone when asked so the other players can see and hear you.
